CII OBLITERATED — UNIDENTIFIED "FORCE DROIDS" ANNIHILATE DISSIDENT FACTION IN SINGLE ENGAGEMENT
◆ BBY 22 — 06:14:02 GST ◆ CORUSCANT BUREAU ◆ JEST ICKLE, CORRESPONDENT

CORUSCANT — In what Republic Intelligence is describing as one of the most sudden and complete military annihilations in the history of the Clone Wars, the Confederación de la Igualdad e Independencia — known across the Outer Rim and among dissident circles under the initials CII — has been utterly destroyed. The organization, which had been growing in size and influence for several standard years, was wiped out in a single engagement against a series of unidentified droid units displaying capabilities that senior Republic analysts and at least two members of the Jedi Council are struggling to classify. The working designation applied to the attackers in classified Senate briefings is "Force Droids." No such designation has any known precedent in the military doctrine of either the Galactic Republic or the Confederacy of Independent Systems.

The CII was not a conventional rebel faction. Its membership included defectors from CIS command — officers who had seen the inner workings of the Separatist machine and found it no less corrupt than the institution it claimed to oppose — alongside soldiers recruited from dozens of worlds throughout the Mid and Outer Rim. Members were distinguished by a striking symbol: a yellow hexagon emblazoned on their armor, a deliberate rejection of both sides' claims to legitimacy. The faction's total membership at the time of its destruction is estimated at somewhere between three and six thousand combatants, supported by an unknown number of civilian sympathizers on at least fourteen worlds.

What made the CII philosophically distinct was its code of conduct. Their doctrine was explicit and genuinely observed: enemies were to be incapacitated, not killed. Lethal force was authorized only when an adversary was deemed an irreducible threat to CII operations. Those captured were transported to CII judicial councils, where a formal legal proceeding determined their fate. Republic officers who had been through this process described it, privately, as "more fair than anything the Senate has produced in a decade."

The faction's founder and supreme leader, Halgane Litole — referred to by his followers simply as Litole — was a human with a background in CIS command before his defection. He held two primary objectives: to expose the Senate's corruption, and to reveal an alleged secret agenda being pursued by the CIS upper echelons. His current status is UNKNOWN. No confirmed identification of his remains has been produced at the engagement site.

The engagement lasted less than four hours. Witnesses described the Force Droids as moving with a quality of awareness that standard battle droids do not possess — anticipating movement before it occurred, deflecting blaster fire through physical repositioning executed at inhuman speed. One witness stated: "They weren't fighting like droids. They were fighting like something that knew what was going to happen before it did." The Jedi Council has been formally notified. Senate hearings are expected within the week.

ANCIENT FURY REBORN — UNIDENTIFIED GEONOSIAN WARRIOR FACTION STRIKES OUTER RIM IN COORDINATED CAMPAIGN
◆ BBY 22 — 08:47:33 GST ◆ OUTER RIM BUREAU — FIELD TRANSMISSION ◆ VINE CALEIS, CORRESPONDENT

OUTER RIM TERRITORIES — A series of coordinated and ferocious attacks across multiple Outer Rim sectors has been attributed to an emerging Geonosian faction whose identity, command structure, and objectives remain almost entirely unknown to Republic forces. What is known — pieced together from survivor accounts, damaged surveillance records, and two Republic patrols who survived contact — paints the portrait of a movement unlike anything the Clone Wars has produced. This faction does not fight like the Separatists. It does not fight like the Republic. It fights like something older.

Clone commanders across seven separate engagement sites have independently used the same phrase: "warrior caste." The attackers are Geonosian — insectoid, winged in some cases, fighting with the ferocity that distinguished ancient Geonosian armies long before the planet became synonymous with droid foundries. Unlike the Geonosian forces encountered during the Clone Wars, which primarily direct battle droid formations from behind the lines, these warriors conduct ground assaults personally and deliberately. Droids are present — the faction operates its own fleet and deploys automated units — but they serve as tools. The killing is done by the warriors themselves. This is not incidental. It is doctrine.

The ideological root of this movement is directed at a specific individual: Poggle the Lesser, Archduke of Geonosis. In selling Geonosis wholesale to the CIS war machine — converting its tunnels, its hives, its ancient arenas into droid production facilities — Poggle did not elevate his people. He erased them. He turned a civilization of warriors into a factory floor. This faction will not forgive it. Their attacks on Separatist supply lines have been as aggressive as their attacks on Republic forces — they do not distinguish between the two powers, except insofar as both represent the political order they have rejected.

Their stated purpose, communicated openly to at least one Republic officer taken briefly as a prisoner then released unharmed: to end the Clone Wars — not through diplomacy, but by dismantling both sides. Beyond that, they intend to rule the galaxy and to retake Geonosis. These are not the ambitions of a resistance movement. They are the ambitions of a power that considers itself the legitimate inheritor of galactic authority.

At the center of this faction is a queen. Her name, as rendered by Republic xenolinguistics specialists from intercepted Geonosian transmissions, is Yamilei. She has not been confirmed present at any engagement. One officer who survived brief capture described being brought before a presence he never directly saw, receiving a message: the Republic should consider what it has done to worlds like Geonosis, because it will be held accountable. He was then returned to his unit, unharmed. Glory, as one recovered Geonosian text states, is not measured by what you control. It is measured by what you defeat. In combat. Personally. The old way. This correspondent advises extreme caution in all flagged Outer Rim sectors. This story is just beginning.
