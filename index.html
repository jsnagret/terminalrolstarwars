═══ SUBJECT PROFILE ═══

The CII is an independent organization operating outside Republic and CIS control. They focus on intelligence gathering, tribunals, and exposing corruption rather than territorial conquest.

═══ IDEOLOGY & DOCTRINE ═══
  • Expose corruption and mismanagement within Republic and Separatist forces
  • Capture high-value targets for tribunal rather than conquest
  • Minimize civilian casualties and collateral damage
  • Prefer incapacitation over lethal force whenever possible

ROLE: Strategic Director / Tribunal Overseer

Secondary operative known as “The Architect”—identity unknown. Provides historical intelligence and operational planning support.

═══ MILITARY & TECHNOLOGY ═══
  • Remote-controlled starfighters and small strike craft
  • Support frigates and transport vessels
  • Autonomous drones for reconnaissance or security
  • Encryption and data-slicing tools for intelligence gathering

Forces are disciplined and prioritize completing objectives with minimal casualties. Engagements are surgical and efficient.

═══ KNOWN OPERATIONS ═══
  • Data extraction from abandoned Republic facilities
  • Infiltration of industrial or corporate sites controlled by Republic or Hutt interests
  • Capture of high-value targets for interrogation or tribunal
═══ INTELLIGENCE NOTES ═══

The CII does not behave like a conventional insurgency. Their operations focus on evidence collection and accountability. They are not a military-first organization but can execute precise strikes when needed.

Recommendation: Monitor activity closely, prioritize intelligence gathering, avoid direct confrontation unless unavoidable.
